The savoury one

The Dagel.

A doughnut that thinks it is a bagel. The same brioche, filled with whipped cream cheese and scallions and finished with everything seasoning — savoury, not sweet, and lighter than the bagel it is impersonating.

There is one savoury slot on every board. Past versions have included Smoked Salmon, Elote and Pimento Cheese.
